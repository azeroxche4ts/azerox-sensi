Method

Three inputs is all the engine needs

AZEROX-SENSI doesn't ask you to fill out a survey. It reads the same three variables a coach would check before adjusting your settings by hand: what screen you're touching, how your thumb actually plays, and which optics you fight through.

01 / DEVICE

Screen & chipset class

Panel size, touch sampling rate, and refresh rate change how far a thumb travels per degree of turn — the engine scales base sensitivity to your device tier before anything else.

02 / PLAYSTYLE

How you actually fight

One-tap players get a tighter, high-drag profile for pixel-precise flicks. Rushers get faster general turn speed. Spray players get flatter scope curves for recoil control.

03 / OPTICS

Scope-by-scope tuning

Red dot, 2x, 4x, and sniper scopes each get an independent value, because a setting that feels right up close usually feels wrong zoomed in.

Why it holds up

Built for consistency, not luck

Random sensitivity advice from clips and comment sections rarely matches your device. AZEROX-SENSI keeps the same profile every time you run it for a given device and playstyle, so you can actually build muscle memory around it.
